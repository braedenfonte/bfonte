Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Jarvis is a personal app used only by its owner, Braeden Fonte. This policy explains how it handles data.

What Jarvis accesses

With the owner's permission, Jarvis can read his Gmail messages, Google Drive files and Google Calendar events, create email drafts, and send emails only after he approves each one.

How the data is used

Data is used only to answer the owner's own requests, such as summarizing his inbox or listing his schedule. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. To generate answers, relevant text is sent to the Google Gemini API for processing.

Storage

Sign-in tokens and short notes the owner asks Jarvis to remember are stored privately for the app's use. Email and file contents are not kept after a request is answered.

Google user data

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
